About Us

Block Architecture Studio is a Tehran-based practice dedicated to creating spaces where minimal form meets purposeful design. We believe in crafting environments that respond directly to their context, celebrating the interplay of natural light, raw materials, and spatial purity. Our multidisciplinary team approaches each project as a unique narrative, stripping away the unnecessary to reveal the essential structure beneath.
