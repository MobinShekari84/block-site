Damas Villa

Residential, Damavand, 2026

Block Architecture Studio designed Damas Villa as a harmonious sanctuary that bridges the rugged topography of Damavand with minimalist residential comfort. The defining characteristic of the project is its strict geometric purity, offset by expansive glazing that invites natural light deep into the core spaces. The material palette—comprising raw concrete, white plaster, and subtle metallic accents—ensures the architecture acts as a quiet canvas for the changing seasons.

Block Architecture Studio

Block Architecture Studio is a Tehran-based practice dedicated to creating spaces where minimal form meets purposeful design.
