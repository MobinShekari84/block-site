01 / PROJECT NARRATIVE

A modern, minimalist office renovation designed for Dr. Pour Abedi. The project was proposed in two scenarios (minimal and maximal) to balance budget and scope. The final design prioritizes open workspaces, natural lighting, and elegant acoustic glass partitions. The material palette features warm oak wood, smoked glass, black metal accents, and a neutral base of warm white and light gray.
